## Proper mortar matching and workmanship

Here is the detail most homeowners never think to ask about — and it matters more than almost anything. Mortar is not one product. It varies in strength, color, and texture, and using the wrong mix can trap moisture or actually crack the surrounding brick over time. Emerald matches mortar to the **original** so repairs blend in and perform correctly, especially on older and historic buildings where the wrong mix does visible, lasting damage. That is the difference between a repair that disappears and one that stands out for the next 20 years.

> The right mortar is invisible when it is done well and obvious when it is done wrong. Matching it is craftsmanship, not guesswork.
