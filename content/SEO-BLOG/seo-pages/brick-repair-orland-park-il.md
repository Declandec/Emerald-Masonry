## What Brick Repair Covers in Orland Park

**Brick repair** in Orland Park usually falls into a few categories, and most walls need a combination:

- **Spalled brick** — bricks whose faces have flaked, popped, or crumbled off. These have to be cut out and replaced; once the hard outer face is gone the brick soaks up water fast.
- **Cracked brick** — bricks split by settlement, rust-jacking from a corroding lintel, or freeze-thaw stress. Hairline cracks may only need repointing; bricks cracked clean through get replaced.
- **Loose or displaced brick** — bricks that have shifted out of plane, often above windows, at corners, or along a bulging section. We reset them true and repoint.
- **Color- and texture-matched replacement** — new brick sourced to match the size, color, and texture of your existing wall so repairs disappear instead of announcing themselves.
- **Repointing around repairs** — raking out and refilling the failed mortar joints next to the repair so the patch is fully bonded and watertight.

### Brick Repair vs. Brick Replacement

People often ask which they need. **Brick repair** keeps sound bricks in place and corrects what's failing — resetting displaced brick, repointing open joints, sealing the water path. [Brick replacement](/services/brick-replacement) means removing units that are cracked through, spalling, or crumbling and setting matched new brick in their place. On a typical Orland Park home you'll see both on the same wall: a few bricks replaced, the joints around them repointed, and the sound field left untouched. We walk you through each area before any work begins so you know exactly what you're paying for.

## Signs Your Orland Park Brick Needs Repair

Watch for these, especially on the north and west sides of the house where our winters hit hardest:

- **Flaking or popping brick faces** — the classic sign of spalling and freeze-thaw damage.
- **Cracked or loose bricks** — cracks running through brick or across several courses, or bricks you can wiggle by hand.
- **Open or crumbling mortar joints** — gaps where mortar has receded, letting water straight into the wall.
- **Step cracks** — stair-step cracks climbing through the mortar joints, often a sign of settlement or a shifting lintel.
- **Efflorescence** — white chalky residue on the brick, which means water is moving through the masonry and evaporating on the surface.

> If you can slide a coin into a mortar joint or a brick face has crumbled away, water is already getting in. That's the moment to call — not next spring.

## The Risk of Waiting

Brick problems don't hold still. One spalled brick lets water reach the two beside it, and next winter you have three. Open joints funnel water behind the wall, where it corrodes lintels and freezes inside the masonry, pushing courses out of line. A step crack left alone can widen into a section that has to be rebuilt instead of repaired. Catching brick repair early in Orland Park almost always means a smaller, cheaper job than waiting until a whole section is compromised.

## Our Brick Repair Process

Emerald Masonry LLC follows the same disciplined sequence on every Orland Park brick repair:

1. **Assess** — we inspect the wall, find every failing brick and joint, and identify *why* it failed. Fixing the brick without fixing the cause just buys you a season.
2. **Match** — we source replacement brick to match size, color, and texture, and mix mortar tinted to match your existing joints.
3. **Cut out** — we carefully remove the failed brick without damaging the sound bricks around it.
4. **Reset** — we set the matched brick true to the wall, keeping courses and joint lines consistent.
5. **Repoint** — we repoint the surrounding joints so the repair is fully bonded and the wall sheds water as one surface.
6. **Fix the water path** — we address the source, whether that's failed joints above, a leaking sill, or a corroded lintel, so the repair lasts.
