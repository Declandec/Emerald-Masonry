## Why tuckpointing in Palos Hills follows the terrain

When we searched for tuckpointing in Palos Hills, the results were almost entirely directory listings and aggregator profiles. None said anything about the town itself. That matters, because Palos Hills homes do not wear like homes on a flat Chicago grid.

The town sits on rolling ground near the Palos forest preserves, with streets that climb and lots that fall away to the back. Three things follow from that:

- **Elevated lots catch more wind-driven rain.** A house on higher, open ground takes rain sideways on its west and south-west walls. Water is pushed into the joints rather than running down the face.
- **Sloped lots expose more brick.** Walk-out and look-out lower levels put a full storey of brick or block close to grade, where snow sits, splash hits and the wall stays damp.
- **Chimneys on high ground get the worst exposure** of any masonry on the property, above the roofline on every side.

## Signs your Palos Hills home needs tuckpointing

- **Mortar recessed a quarter-inch or more** behind the brick face, most often on the weather side first.
- **Sandy or powdery joints** that scrape out with a key or screwdriver.
- **Stair-step cracks** at window corners and along the exposed lower level.
- **White efflorescence** on walk-out walls and below downspouts.
- **Loose top courses or open joints on the chimney**, visible from the street or with binoculars.
- **Water stains** inside on the lower level, or below windows on the weather wall.

## What happens if you wait

Open joints let water into the wall. In a Chicagoland winter that water freezes and expands, the joints erode faster each season, and eventually the brick faces start to **spall** (flake and break off). Tuckpointing is far cheaper than replacing brick, so the time to repoint is when the joints are recessed, not when the brick is breaking. On a chimney, waiting can mean a rebuild above the roofline instead of a repoint.

## How we tuckpoint homes in Palos Hills

1. **Survey every wall.** We check joint depth and soundness elevation by elevation and mark which walls need work now.
2. **Check the water first.** Gutters, downspouts, grade against walk-out walls, and weep holes on veneer homes, so repointing is not asked to fix a drainage problem.
3. **Match the mortar.** We record colour, sand and joint profile and make cured sample patches for your approval.
4. **Cut out failed mortar** to a depth of at least twice the joint width, without chipping brick edges.
5. **Repoint in layers** with matched **Type N mortar**, never harder than the brick.
6. **Tool the joints** to the original profile so repaired walls blend with sound ones.
7. **Clean, protect and cure**, shading fresh mortar from fast drying and keeping it above freezing.

## Materials we use

Pre-blended or site-batched **Type N** mortar matched in colour and sand to your existing joints; **Type S** only for below-grade or heavily exposed block where it suits the masonry; matched replacement brick where units have cracked; and a breathable **siloxane water repellent** only where a wall genuinely needs one after repointing. We do not put film-forming sealers on brick.
