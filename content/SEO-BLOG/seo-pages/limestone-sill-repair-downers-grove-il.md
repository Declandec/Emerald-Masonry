## Limestone sill repair in Downers Grove starts with identifying the sill

When we searched for limestone sill repair in Downers Grove, the results were interior stone polishers and countertop restorers. None of them deal with an exterior window sill set in a brick wall. Here is how the four common sill types compare:

| Sill type | How to recognise it | How it usually fails | Typical repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Natural limestone** | Fine natural texture, slight colour variation, sometimes fossils | Cracks, surface flaking, eroded edges | Pin and patch, stone repair mortar, or replace with new limestone |
| **Cast stone** | Uniform colour, fine sandy surface | Cracking, corners breaking off, rusting rods inside | Patch small damage; replace if rods are rusting |
| **Precast concrete** | Grey, plain, may show air holes or exposed rods | Cracks, spalling over rusting steel | Replace with new precast or limestone |
| **Brick rowlock sill** | Bricks set on edge, sloped | Open joints, loose or spalled bricks | Repoint, reset or replace bricks |

## Signs your window sills need repair

- **Cracks** through the sill, often near the middle or at a joint.
- **Flaking or crumbling** surface on limestone or cast stone.
- **A sill sloping back** toward the window instead of away from it.
- **Open joints** at the sill ends or between the sill and the window frame.
- **Rust stains** or exposed steel in a cast stone or concrete sill.
- **Stained or cracked brick** under the sill, or water inside below the window.

## Risks of waiting

A failed sill sends water into the brick below, where freeze-thaw cracks the mortar and spalls the brick. Water at the window frame rots wood frames and interior trim. Rusting steel inside a cast stone or concrete sill keeps expanding until the sill breaks. Repointing and resealing a sound sill costs far less than replacing the sill and the brick under it.

## How we repair sills in Downers Grove

1. **Identify the sill** and check how it is set, sloped and jointed.
