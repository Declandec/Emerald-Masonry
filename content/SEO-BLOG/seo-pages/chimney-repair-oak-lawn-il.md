## What Chimney Repair Covers

Chimney repair isn't one task — it's a set of fixes matched to how and where a chimney is breaking down. When Emerald Masonry LLC handles **chimney repair in Oak Lawn**, we commonly perform:

- **Chimney tuckpointing** — grinding out failed mortar joints and repacking them with fresh, color-matched mortar. This is the most common repair and, done early, it can add decades of life.
- **Above-roofline rebuilds** — when brick above the roof is loose, spalled, or leaning, we take the chimney down to a sound course and rebuild it with matched brick and proper mortar.
- **Chimney crown repair** — the concrete or masonry cap at the very top sheds water away from the flue. Cracked crowns let water pour straight into the structure, so we repair or rebuild them.
- **Flashing repair** — the metal seal where the chimney meets the roof is a frequent leak point. We reseal or replace flashing to stop water intrusion.
- **Spalled brick replacement** — flaking, pitting, and popping brick faces get swapped out before the damage spreads.
- **Chimney caps** — a proper cap keeps out rain, animals, and debris while letting the flue breathe.

If you're not sure which of these your chimney needs, that's exactly what our free inspection is for.

## Signs Your Oak Lawn Chimney Needs Repair

Most chimney failures give plenty of warning if you know what to look for. Watch for:

- **White staining (efflorescence)** on the brick — a sign water is moving through the masonry.
- **Crumbling or missing mortar** between the joints.
- **A leaning or tilting chimney** — often a sign the mortar has failed or the crown is letting water into the core.
- **Brick or mortar debris** on the roof, in gutters, or on the ground around the base.
- **Leaks or water stains** on the ceiling or wall near the chimney chase.

Any one of these is worth a call. Two or more usually means the chimney is actively deteriorating and shouldn't wait.

## The Risks of Waiting

It's tempting to put chimney work off, but a deteriorating chimney rarely stays the same — it gets worse, and the cost of fixing it climbs with it. Falling brick is a genuine safety hazard to people and property below. A leaning chimney can eventually collapse. And once water gets past failed mortar or a cracked crown, it doesn't stop at the brick — it rots roof framing, ruins interior ceilings and walls, and can damage the flue liner. A tuckpointing job handled this year is almost always far cheaper than a rebuild plus interior repairs next year.

## Materials We Use

Good chimney repair is about matching what's already there. We use **Type N and Type S mortar**, selected for the strength and flexibility your specific masonry needs, and we tint it to match your existing joints. For rebuilds and brick replacement, we source **matched brick** so the repair disappears into the original wall. We repair or rebuild **crowns** to properly shed water and reseal or replace **flashing** so the roof-to-chimney joint stays watertight. Using the wrong mortar — one that's harder than the surrounding brick — can actually accelerate damage, which is why the right material selection matters as much as the workmanship.
