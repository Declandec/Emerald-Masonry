## What Brick Repair Covers

Brick repair is more than swapping a bad brick. On a typical Westchester repair we handle:

- **Spalled brick** — brick whose faces have flaked, popped, or crumbled from freeze-thaw and trapped moisture. We cut out the failed units and replace them.
- **Cracked brick** — stepped or vertical cracks that run through brick and mortar, often a sign of movement, a failing lintel, or water damage.
- **Loose or displaced brick** — brick that has shifted out of plane, especially around windows, sills, steps, and near grade. We reset displaced brick back into the wall line.
- **Color- and texture-matched replacement** — sourcing the closest brick match by size, color, and surface texture so the repair disappears once it weathers in.
- **Repointing around the repair** — replacing deteriorated mortar joints next to the patch so the whole section is watertight, not just the new brick.

For larger sections where whole courses have failed, [brick replacement](/services/brick-replacement) is the better call; where the brick is sound but the joints are the problem, [tuckpointing](/services/tuckpointing) restores the mortar. We will tell you which one your wall actually needs.

## Signs Your Westchester Brick Needs Repair

Walk the outside of your home and look for:

- White chalky faces or brick that flakes when you rub it (spalling)
- Chips and popped faces, especially on north and west walls
- Cracks that step diagonally through the mortar joints
- Brick you can wiggle by hand, or a bulge in the wall line
- Crumbling, sandy, or missing mortar between brick
- Damp interior walls or efflorescence (white staining) after rain or snowmelt

Any one of these near grade, under windows, or on a chimney is worth a look. Catching it while it is one or two brick keeps it from becoming a wall.

## The Risks of Waiting

Brick problems do not stay small. A single spalled brick lets water into the wall behind it. In Westchester's freeze-thaw winters that water freezes, expands, and pushes the damage into neighboring brick and mortar. What starts as one flaking face can, over a couple of seasons, become a soft, saturated section that needs far more brick pulled out — and once water reaches the structure behind the veneer, the repair gets bigger and more expensive. Fixing a small area now is almost always the cheaper path.

> "The cheapest brick repair is the one you do while it is still small." — a rule we live by on every Westchester job.

## Our Brick Repair Process

We keep it straightforward and honest:

1. **Assess** — We inspect the wall on-site, identify what has failed versus what is still sound, and find the cause (moisture, movement, a lintel, or age) so the repair actually lasts.
2. **Match** — We source brick that matches your existing color, size, and texture, and blend mortar to match your joint color and profile.
3. **Cut out** — We carefully remove only the failed brick, protecting the surrounding good masonry.
4. **Reset & replace** — We set the new or displaced brick back into the wall line, plumb and true.
5. **Repoint** — We repoint the joints around the repair so the section is watertight and the patch blends into the wall.

You get a clear recommendation and a free estimate in writing before we start — no surprises.

## Materials: Matching Matters

On homes this age, the original brick is often out of production, so a good match is the whole game. We match by **color, unit size, and surface texture**, and we tune the **mortar color and joint profile** to your existing wall so the repair reads as part of the house — not a patch. Getting the mortar right matters as much as the brick: the wrong color or a mismatched joint stands out more than the brick itself.
