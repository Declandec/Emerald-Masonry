## Access Is a Line Item, Not an Assumption

| Method | Suits | What it costs you |
|---|---|---|
| **Ladder / ground work** | Single-storey, low elevations | Cheapest, very limited reach |
| **Scissor lift** | Flat, firm ground, moderate height | Fast to reposition; needs a route and standing room |
| **Boom lift** | Upper elevations, obstructions below | Reach over landscaping and parked cars; larger footprint |
| **Pipe staging** | Long elevations, detailed work, tight sites | Days to erect and dismantle; best for large continuous scopes |
| **Overhead protection** | Any work above a walkway or entrance | Built before cutting begins; non-optional where required |

A scope that says "contractor to provide access" without naming the method is inviting three incomparable bids. Ours states the method, who provides it, and what it assumes about the ground and the route.

## Where Joints Fail First

- **Parapets and coping joints** — exposed on both faces, and standing over walkways
- **South and west elevations** — driving rain and the widest temperature swings
- **Below sills, copings and projections**, where water runs back onto the wall
- **At and above lintels and shelf angles**, where corroding steel opens the joint from behind
- **Base courses** — splash-back, snow, and de-icing salt
- **Around penetrations** — vents, conduit, sign anchors and abandoned fastener holes

## Our Process

1. **Assess every elevation and the roof edge**, from a lift or staging rather than the ground.
2. **Sound and probe** — drummy masonry and true joint depth do not photograph.
3. **Write the scope**: quantities by elevation, cut-out depth, mortar specification, access method, protection, phasing, and stated assumptions.
4. **Plan the site** — lift routes, staging footprint, parking and drive-aisle impact, tenant notice, working hours.
5. **Cut out to sound material** by grinding or hand-raking, without damaging unit edges.
6. **Repoint in matched mortar** — colour, sand, texture and hardness matched to the units.
7. **Tool the joints** to the existing profile at the right point in the set.
8. **Address sealant, flashing and weeps** where the assessment found the real water path.
9. **Clean down and walk it with you**, elevation by elevation.
