## What Chimney Repair Covers

"Chimney repair" is really a handful of distinct repairs, and a good inspection sorts out which ones yours needs:

- **Crown repair or recasting** — the sloped concrete cap on top that sheds water away from the flue and brick. Cracked crowns are the number-one source of chimney leaks.
- **Repointing** — grinding out and replacing failed mortar joints in the brick stack.
- **Flashing** — the metal that seals the joint where the chimney passes through the roof; a frequent leak source (we handle the masonry side).
- **Brick replacement** — swapping spalled or cracked brick before the damage spreads.
- **Partial or full rebuild** — taking the stack down to a sound course and rebuilding when the masonry has failed structurally.
- **Chimney cap** — a metal cap over the flue that keeps out rain, animals, and debris.

## Signs Your Orland Park Chimney Needs Attention

- **Pieces of mortar or brick** on the roof or in the yard
- **A cracked, crumbling, or missing crown** at the top
- **White efflorescence** staining on the chimney brick
- **Spalling** — brick faces flaking, popping, or pitting
- **Water stains** on the ceiling or wall around the chimney chase
- **A visible lean or bow** in the stack — a structural warning sign
- **Rusted or lifted flashing** where the chimney meets the roof

## The Risk of Waiting

A chimney problem is a water problem, and water problems compound fast at the roofline. A hairline crack in the crown lets in moisture that freezes, expands, and widens the crack every winter. Open mortar joints do the same. Left alone, that cycle moves the damage from the crown into the brick, from the brick into the structure, and eventually into the roof framing and ceilings. A chimney that needed a crown repair one year can need a full rebuild a few years later. On top of the cost, a deteriorating chimney is a safety issue — leaning stacks and spalled brick can drop material. Early repair is always cheaper and safer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my Orland Park chimney needs repair or a full rebuild?
If the damage is limited to a cracked crown, surface mortar loss, or a few spalled bricks near the top, targeted repair and repointing usually solve it. A rebuild is needed when the stack is leaning, the brick is spalling through multiple courses, or the mortar has failed structurally. Emerald Masonry inspects and tells you honestly which one your chimney needs.

### Why is water leaking near my chimney in Orland Park?
The usual culprits are a cracked chimney crown, failed mortar joints, deteriorated flashing where the chimney meets the roof, or a missing cap. Any of these lets water into the masonry, where freeze-thaw cycles widen the damage. We find the actual entry point rather than sealing over symptoms.

### Can you repair just the chimney crown without rebuilding the whole chimney?
