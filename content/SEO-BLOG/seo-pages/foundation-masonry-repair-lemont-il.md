## Foundation masonry repair in Lemont starts with knowing your foundation

Lemont grew around its **limestone quarries** in the 1800s, and its local stone was shipped across the region. ## Signs your Lemont foundation needs repair

- **Mortar crumbling or missing** between foundation stones or blocks.
- **Loose or shifting stones** in an older foundation wall.
- **Stair-step cracks** in block, especially at corners and below windows.
- **Horizontal cracks** or a wall that looks bowed inward.
- **Parging** that is cracked, hollow or falling off.
- **Damp walls, efflorescence** or water on the basement floor after rain.
- **Rusted lintels** or cracked block around basement windows.

## Risks of waiting

Water is the main enemy of any foundation. Open joints and cracks let it in, and each winter freeze-thaw widens them. On stone foundations, mortar loss lets stones loosen and shift. On block walls, water and soil pressure turn small cracks into bowing. Repointing and crack repair caught early cost far less than rebuilding a section of foundation wall.

## Lemont's sloped lots and foundation masonry repair

Lemont sits on the bluffs above the Des Plaines River valley, and many lots slope. On a sloped lot, runoff and groundwater move toward the uphill side of the house and press against the foundation wall there. That is why uphill walls on Lemont homes are often wetter and more cracked than the others. We look at where water comes from as well as at the wall itself: grading, downspouts, window wells and walk-out walls. A masonry repair lasts much longer when the water is redirected too.
