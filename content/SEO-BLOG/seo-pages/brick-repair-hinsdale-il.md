## Signs your Hinsdale home needs brick repair

- **Brick faces flaking or crumbling**, especially low on walls, at chimneys or under gutters.
- **Cracks** through brick, at window and door corners, or where brick meets stone.
- **Loose bricks** at corners, steps or the tops of walls.
- **Missing or blocked weep holes** along the bottom course of veneer walls.
- **Bulging or leaning** areas on a veneer wall.
- **Rust staining** above openings.
- **Patches** in hard gray mortar or mismatched brick on older homes.

## Risks of waiting

On a solid wall, damaged brick takes in more water each year and freeze-thaw spreads the damage. On a veneer wall, trapped water corrodes the ties that hold the brick to the house and can damage the sheathing and framing behind it, problems that stay hidden until the brick cracks or bulges. Either way, a contained repair becomes a larger rebuild with time.

## How we repair brick in Hinsdale

1. **Identify the wall type** and inspect inside and out where possible.
2. **Find the cause**: water entry, tie corrosion, flashing, lintels, hard mortar or movement.
3. **Source and show the match**: current, salvaged or reclaimed brick and a mortar sample.
4. **Remove damaged brick** without disturbing the surrounding wall.
5. **On solid walls**, rebuild in compatible lime-rich or Type N mortar, bonded to the layers behind.
6. **On veneer walls**, replace corroded ties, repair flashing and reopen or add weep holes.
7. **Repair cracks at openings**, including rusted lintels where needed.
8. **Tool and clean** the joints to blend in, and walk through the work with you.

## Materials we use

Matched face brick, salvaged brick of the same era for older homes, or brick reclaimed from a hidden area of the house; lime-rich or Type N mortar matched to the original on older brick; Type N or S on veneer as the wall requires; corrosion-resistant veneer ties; flexible through-wall flashing and weep vents; and primed or galvanized steel lintels. We never use mortar harder than the brick, and we do not seal over cracks.
