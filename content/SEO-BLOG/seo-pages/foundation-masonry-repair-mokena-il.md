> On a brick-veneer home, the foundation and the brick are connected. A crack below often shows up in the brick above first.

## How the foundation and the brick connect

On most newer Mokena homes, the basement is **poured concrete**, and the brick veneer sits on a **brick ledge**, a step formed along the top of the foundation wall. On older homes, the foundation may be **concrete block** with brick or siding above. Either way, the foundation carries the brick, so movement or cracking in one tends to show up in the other.

That makes the line where foundation meets brick one of the most useful places to look on the outside of a house.

## Foundation masonry repair in Mokena: which cracks matter

| What you see | What it often means | Typical next step |
|---|---|---|
| Hairline vertical crack in foundation and brick, unchanged | Concrete shrinkage or old settling | Seal, repoint, monitor |
| Stair-step crack in brick starting at the foundation | Minor settlement at a corner | Repair brick and joints, check drainage |
| Crack wider at the top than the bottom | Ongoing settlement | Structural engineer first |
| Crack wider than about a quarter inch, or growing | Active movement | Structural engineer first |
| Cracked brick or block at a window well | Water in the well or unsupported opening | Rebuild masonry, lintel, drainage |
| Horizontal crack or bowing in a block wall | Soil and water pressure | Engineer's review, then repair |
| Parging cracked or falling off | Water behind it, or wrong mix | Remove loose parging, repair, re-parge |

## Signs your Mokena foundation masonry needs repair

- **Cracks at the base of the brick**, especially at corners and under windows.
- **Gaps** between the brick and the foundation ledge.
- **Cracked or loose brick or block** around window wells and egress openings.
- **Parging** that is cracked, hollow or flaking.
- **Damp basement walls** or efflorescence near cracks.
- **Mortar washed out** of the bottom courses of brick.
- **Downspouts discharging** right next to the foundation.

## Risks of waiting

Water follows cracks. Each winter it freezes in them, widening the gap in the foundation and the brick above. Water in window wells soaks the surrounding masonry and leaks into the basement. A crack that could be sealed and repointed can become a section of brick to rebuild, and leaks can damage finished basements.

## How we repair foundation masonry in Mokena

1. **Inspect inside and out**, and measure and photograph each crack.
2. **Recommend an engineer** if the crack pattern points to active movement.
