## What foundation masonry repair covers

**Foundation masonry repair** fixes the masonry part of a foundation: the concrete block, brick or stone walls below and just above grade. It includes crack repair, repointing failed joints, replacing damaged block or brick, rebuilding sections that have bowed or shifted, and repairing the parging (the cement coat) on exposed walls. It doesn't include poured-concrete crack injection or underpinning, and we'll tell you if a specialist or engineer is needed.

## Signs your Palos Park foundation needs repair

- **Stair-step cracks** in block walls, especially on the uphill side.
- **Horizontal cracks** along a mortar joint partway up the wall.
- **A wall bowing inward** when you sight along it from a corner.
- **Water seeping** through joints or cracks after heavy rain or snowmelt.
- **Efflorescence**, the white salt deposits on basement walls.
- **Crumbling parging** on the exposed part of a walk-out wall.
- **Loose or missing mortar** in older stone or brick foundations.
- **Sticking doors and windows** on the floors above.

## Foundation masonry repair in Palos Park: why sloped lots are different

| Condition on a Palos Park lot | What it does to the foundation |
|---|---|
| Uphill wall with soil against its full height | Highest sideways pressure, most likely to crack or bow |
| Walk-out wall exposed on the downhill side | Weather on the outside face, freeze-thaw on the parging |
| Water running down the slope toward the house | Saturated soil, leaks and frost pressure |
| Gutters clogged with leaves from mature trees | Overflow dumped right at the foundation |
| Large trees close to the house | Seasonal soil shrinkage and roots following cracks |
| Older stone or rubble foundations | Soft lime mortar washing out of joints |

## Risks of waiting

Foundation problems on a slope rarely stand still. Every wet spring adds water and every winter adds frost pressure against the uphill wall. A crack that could be repointed this year can turn into a bowed section that needs rebuilding. Water getting in damages finished basements and invites mold.

## Our foundation repair process in Palos Park

1. **Inspect inside and out**: crack patterns, any bowing, the grade, gutters and downspouts, and where the water is coming from.
2. **Bring in an engineer** if a wall is bowing more than a little or a crack is offset.
3. **Excavate** the outside of the wall where needed, working carefully around trees and landscaping.
4. **Repair the masonry**: repoint joints, replace cracked block, or rebuild bowed sections with filled and reinforced block.
5. **Waterproof and drain**: damp-proof the outside face, and add or repair drain tile where it's missing.
6. **Backfill and regrade** so the soil slopes away from the house.
7. **Fix gutters and downspouts** so the water ends up away from the foundation.

## Materials we use

Concrete block with grouted, reinforced cores for rebuilt sections; **Type S mortar** for below-grade block; softer **lime-based mortar** for older stone and brick foundations; cementitious parging for exposed walls; damp-proofing membranes; and drainage stone and pipe. We match the mortar to the wall, because hard mortar in old stone causes more damage.

## What drives the cost of foundation masonry repair

- **The repair needed**: crack repair, repointing or rebuilding a section.
- **Excavation**: how deep and how much, and what's in the way (patios, steps, landscaping, trees).
- **Access**: sloped and wooded lots can limit equipment.
- **Drainage work**: drain tile, regrading and downspout extensions.
- **Engineering**, where a wall has moved enough to need it.
