## Signs You Need Foundation Masonry Repair in Lockport

Foundations tend to warn you before they fail. On Lockport homes, these are the signs worth acting on:

- **Crumbling or receding mortar** in the foundation courses — you can scrape it out with a screwdriver.
- **Step cracks** climbing diagonally through the mortar joints of block or brick.
- **Spalling** — brick or block faces flaking, popping, or breaking away, especially near grade where snow and splash-back sit.
- **Efflorescence** — a chalky white mineral bloom on the foundation, which means water is moving through the masonry.
- **A flaking or blistering parge coat** peeling off the exposed block.
- **Water seepage or damp spots** on the inside of the foundation wall.

Any one of these means moisture is getting into the wall or the mortar has lost its grip. Together, they mean it's time for a look.

### Why Waiting Costs More

A single deteriorated joint is cheap to repoint. Left alone, water works into that joint, freezes, and expands — Lockport goes through dozens of freeze-thaw cycles every winter — prying the joint wider and loosening the brick or block around it. What starts as a bit of soft mortar becomes lost units, a compromised parge coat, and water in the basement. Foundation masonry is one of the clearest cases where a small, timely repair prevents a large, expensive one.

## Our Foundation Repair Process

1. **Assess.** We inspect the foundation inside and out, identify whether you're looking at masonry deterioration or a structural issue, and pinpoint how far the damage runs.
2. **Rake and repoint.** We grind out the deteriorated mortar to a sound depth and repoint with mortar matched to your foundation.
3. **Replace damaged units.** Cracked or spalled brick and block are cut out and replaced with sound, matching units.
4. **Re-parge.** Where the parge coat has failed, we restore a clean cement coating so the exposed wall sheds water.
5. **Recommend water management.** Because water is almost always the root cause, we'll point out grading, gutter, and downspout fixes that keep water off the foundation for good.

## Materials We Use

We repoint foundations with the correct **Type N or Type S mortar** for the wall's exposure and strength, replace units with matching **brick or CMU block**, and re-parge with a proper cement **parge coat**. Matching the mortar to the masonry matters here just as much as it does above grade — mortar that's too hard can damage older brick as the wall moves and freezes.
