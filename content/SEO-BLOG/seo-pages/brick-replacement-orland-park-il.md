## Replace, repair or stain: brick replacement in Orland Park starts with a diagnosis

| What your brick looks like | What it needs |
|---|---|
| Sound brick, faded or patchy colour | **Staining** may be right, after any mortar repairs |
| Sound brick, worn or open mortar joints | **Tuckpointing**, not replacement |
| Hairline crack through the mortar only | **Brick repair**: repoint the joint and watch it |
| Face of the brick flaking, popping or crumbling | **Brick replacement** |
| Crack running through the brick itself | **Brick replacement**, and find the cause of the crack |
| Bricks broken by a car, snowblower or ladder | **Brick replacement** |
| Many cracked bricks over a window or garage door | **Lintel repair** first, then replacement |

Most Orland Park homes need a mix. A few spalled bricks at the garage piers, some open joints on the weather side, and a cracked area over a window are a typical visit.

## Signs you need brick replacement

- **Spalling**: the face of the brick has popped off, leaving rough, sandy brick behind.
- **Flaking or powdering** brick near the ground, around hose bibs or under downspouts.
- **Cracks through the brick**, not just through the mortar.
- **Broken corners** on garage piers and along the driveway.
- **Loose brick** you can move by hand.
- **Old patches** in grey mortar or the wrong brick that stand out on the front of the house.

## Why Orland Park brick spalls: the usual causes

Brick spalls when it soaks up water and the water freezes. On Orland Park's suburban homes, the water usually comes from one of these:

- **Lawn sprinklers** spraying the same section of wall every summer evening.
- **Downspouts** emptying against the foundation and splashing the bottom courses.
- **Salted snow** shovelled or plowed against the brick beside the driveway and walk.
- **Failed caulk and flashing** around windows and doors letting water into the wall.
- **Soft or under-fired brick** in some builder blends, which absorbs more water than others.
- **Paint or a sealer** that has trapped moisture behind the face of the brick.

Replacing the brick without fixing the cause means replacing it again in a few winters. We point out the cause and include the fix in the scope.

## Risks of waiting

Spalled brick soaks up more water than sound brick, so the damage speeds up each winter. Water that gets past the face reaches the ties and sheathing behind veneer walls and the steel lintels over openings. A dozen bricks this year can become a section of wall in a few years.

## How we replace brick in Orland Park

1. **Find the cause** of the damage, and check the lintels, sills and flashing nearby.
2. **Match the brick.** We take a sample to find the nearest current blend, look for salvaged brick, or borrow original brick from a hidden spot for small areas.
3. **Show you samples** against the wall before any brick is set.
4. **Cut out the damaged bricks** without disturbing the ones around them, keeping ties and weep holes intact.
5. **Set the new brick** in mortar matched for colour and joint profile.
6. **Fix the cause**: redirect downspouts, reset sprinklers, recaulk or reflash.
7. **Clean up and walk the work with you.**

## Materials we use

Matched face brick or salvaged brick; **Type N mortar** colour-matched to the existing joints; new ties where old ones have rusted; through-wall flashing and weep vents where they're missing; and sealant at window and door perimeters. We don't apply a film-forming sealer over brick that needs to dry.
