## What foundation masonry repair in Worth covers

Foundation masonry repair is the repair of the block, brick and mortar that carry the house and keep the basement dry. On most homes in Worth that means a **concrete block (CMU) foundation**, with brick starting a little above grade. It does not include pushing a bowed wall back with anchors or piers, which is structural work; if we see that, we say so.

## Why foundations in Worth fail at the basement windows

A basement window cuts a hole in the foundation wall. The block around it carries load differently, and the opening collects water from above and from the window well outside. Three things follow:

- **Cracks run from the corners.** Settlement and soil pressure concentrate at the opening, so step cracks start at the window corners.
- **Steel over the opening rusts.** Many basement windows have a steel lintel or angle above them. When it rusts it expands, pushes the block and brick above, and cracks them.
- **Window wells hold water.** A well without a working drain, or one next to a downspout, keeps the block around the window wet and lets water into the joints below.

## Signs your Worth foundation needs masonry repair

- **Step cracks** in the block, especially from basement window corners.
- **Orange rust stains** or cracking above a basement window.
- **Water coming in** at or below a basement window after rain.
- **Parging** cracked, hollow or falling off the exposed foundation.
- **White efflorescence** on the inside or outside of the block wall.
- **Eroded or missing mortar** in the block joints near grade.
- **A horizontal crack** or inward bow, which needs a closer look before any repair.

## Risks of waiting

Open joints and cracks let water into the wall. Each winter that water freezes, widens the crack and pushes the block further apart. A rusting lintel keeps expanding until the masonry above it breaks. A basement that stays damp damages finishes, belongings and air quality. Repointing and a lintel repair cost far less than replacing block or chasing a wet basement for years.

## How we repair foundations in Worth

1. **Inspect inside and out.** We check every crack, opening and window well and measure whether cracks are active.
2. **Separate water from structure.** We note gutters, downspouts, grade and window-well drains, and flag anything structural for an engineer.
3. **Repair the opening.** Replace or clean, prime and coat the steel lintel, and rebuild the block or brick above it.
4. **Tuckpoint cracks and joints** with matched mortar, replacing cracked or spalled block units.
5. **Re-parge** the exposed foundation band on sound block.
6. **Seal** window-to-masonry joints with a durable sealant and leave weep paths open.
