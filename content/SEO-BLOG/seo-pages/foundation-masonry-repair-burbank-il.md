## Foundation masonry repair in Burbank: where it starts

Burbank's housing went up mostly in the post-war decades, and many homes share the same details: a side or back door with **outside steps down to the basement**, **basement window wells** along the foundation, and **block or poured foundations** now 60 or more years old. These are the three places we see foundation masonry fail most often.

| Where | What fails | What it causes | Typical repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Basement stairwell walls** | Tipping, cracking, washed-out joints | Leaks at the basement door, unsafe steps | Rebuild with drainage behind, new caps |
| **Stairwell steps and landing** | Cracked treads, settled landing, clogged drain | Flooding at the door | Repair or rebuild steps, clear or upgrade drain |
| **Window wells** | Cracked block or brick around the opening, rusted lintel | Leaks at basement windows | Rebuild opening, new lintel, drain the well |
| **Foundation walls** | Stair-step or horizontal cracks, worn joints | Damp walls, efflorescence, leaks | Repoint, replace block, crack repair |
| **Parging** | Cracking, hollow spots, falling off | Exposed block, poor appearance | Remove loose parging, repair and re-parge |

## Signs your Burbank foundation masonry needs repair

- **Stairwell walls leaning** inward or cracked at the corners.
- **Water pooling** at the bottom of the stairwell or under the basement door.
- **Loose or missing caps** on top of stairwell walls.
- **Cracked brick or block** around window wells.
- **Stair-step cracks** in block foundation walls.
- **Damp patches or white efflorescence** on basement walls.
- **Parging** that is cracked, hollow or flaking.

## Risks of waiting

Stairwell walls hold back soil and water. Once they start to tip, every winter pushes them further, until the wall cracks through or the steps become unsafe. Water that gets in at stairwells and window wells soaks basement walls, damages finishes and freezes in cracks. A wall that could be repointed and drained can become one that must be rebuilt.

## How we repair foundation masonry in Burbank

1. **Inspect** the stairwell, window wells and foundation walls inside and out.
2. **Measure cracks** and recommend a structural engineer when the pattern calls for it.
3. **Trace the water**: downspouts, grading, stairwell drains and window wells.
4. **Rebuild stairwell walls** on a sound base, with drainage behind and solid caps on top.
5. **Repair or rebuild steps** and the landing, and clear or upgrade the drain.
6. **Rebuild window well openings** with new lintels where needed.
7. **Repair block walls**: repoint, replace cracked block and repair cracks.
8. **Repair parging** with a compatible mix and walk you through the work.

## Materials we use

Concrete block and matched brick; Type S mortar for below-grade and retaining work and Type N where it fits; solid caps for stairwell walls; drainage stone and fabric behind rebuilt walls; primed or galvanized steel lintels; and compatible parging mixes. We do not cover cracks with surface patching.
