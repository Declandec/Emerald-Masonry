## Brick Repair vs. Tuckpointing — What's the Difference

People use the terms interchangeably, but they are different repairs. **Tuckpointing** fixes the mortar joints *between* brick. **Brick repair** addresses the brick units themselves — replacing spalled or broken brick, repairing cracks, and resetting loose units. Many Carpentersville walls need both, because failed joints are what let the water in that ultimately wrecked the brick. We assess for both during the estimate.

## Signs Your Carpentersville Brick Needs Repair

- **Spalling** — the fired face flaking, popping, or crumbling away, leaving a rough, lighter crater.
- **Cracked brick** — single cracked units, or stair-step cracks climbing through the joints.
- **Loose or bulging brick** — areas pushing out of plane, common at corners, sills, and chimneys.
- **Brick fragments on the ground** below windows, at the foundation, or near the chimney.
- **Rust stains and cracking above windows and doors** — a corroding steel lintel expanding behind the brick.
- **White efflorescence** — a sign water is moving through the wall.

## The Risk of Waiting

Spalling and cracking only move one direction. Once the hard fired face of a brick is gone, the soft interior drinks up water, and Carpentersville's Fox Valley freeze-thaw winters expand it and accelerate the breakdown. One failed brick becomes a cluster; a hairline crack becomes a path for water deeper into the wall. Address it while it is a handful of brick and a water fix, and you avoid the structural rebuild that waiting invites.

## Our Brick Repair Process

1. **Diagnose the cause.** We find why the brick failed — joints, lintel rust, flashing, splashback, or a worn sealer — so the repair lasts.
2. **Remove the failed brick.** We cut out the damaged units cleanly without disturbing the sound surrounding masonry.
3. **Source matching brick.** We match size, color, and texture as closely as the original allows, including salvaged units when needed.
4. **Reset and repoint.** We lay the new brick and point the joints with color- and strength-matched mortar.
5. **Fix the water source.** We repair the lintel, joints, flashing, or sealer that let water in — the step that makes the repair permanent.
6. **Clean down.** We leave the brick face clean and the repair blended into the wall.

## Materials and Brick Matching

The hard part of brick repair is usually the match. Much of Carpentersville's residential stock dates from the mid-century Meadowdale building era and the decades around it, and brick from that period can often be matched closely by dimension, color blend, and texture; for older or discontinued brick we source salvaged units. Just as important, we point with the correct mortar type — a mortar harder than the brick will, over freeze-thaw cycles, push damage right back into the brick face.

**Why is the brick on my Carpentersville home spalling?**

Spalling is water damage. Water gets into the brick, freezes in our Kane County winters, and pops the hard fired face off. The usual sources are failed mortar joints, a worn-off sealer, splashback near grade, or rust expanding behind a steel lintel.
