The chimney is the single most exposed piece of masonry on a Burbank home. It stands above the roofline with weather hitting it from every direction, no overhang to protect it, and it carries the punishment of the freeze-thaw cycle longer than any wall on the house. ## What Chimney Repair Covers

"Chimney repair" is really a handful of distinct repairs, and a good inspection sorts out which ones your chimney actually needs:

- **Crown repair or recasting** — the crown is the sloped concrete cap on top that sheds water away from the flue and brick. Cracked crowns are the number-one source of chimney leaks.
- **Repointing** — grinding out and replacing failed mortar joints in the brick stack, the most common ongoing maintenance a chimney needs.
- **Flashing** — the metal that seals the joint where the chimney passes through the roof. Failed or improperly installed flashing is a frequent leak source (we coordinate the masonry side of this).
- **Brick replacement** — swapping out spalled or cracked brick before the damage spreads.
- **Partial or full rebuild** — taking the stack down to a sound course and rebuilding when the masonry has failed structurally.
- **Chimney cap** — a metal cap over the flue that keeps rain, animals, and debris out.

## Signs Your Burbank Chimney Needs Attention

- **Pieces of mortar or brick** found on the roof or in the yard
- **A cracked, crumbling, or missing crown** at the top of the chimney
- **White efflorescence** staining on the chimney brick
- **Spalling** — brick faces flaking, popping, or pitting
- **Water stains** on the ceiling or wall around the chimney chase
- **A visible lean** or bow in the stack — this is a structural warning sign
- **Rusted or lifted flashing** where the chimney meets the roof

## The Risk of Waiting

A chimney problem is a water problem, and water problems compound fast at roofline. A hairline crack in the crown lets in moisture that freezes, expands, and widens the crack every winter. Open mortar joints do the same. Left alone, that cycle moves the damage from the crown into the brick, from the brick into the structure, and eventually into your roof framing and ceilings. A chimney that needed a $-bracket crown repair one year can need a full rebuild a few years later. On top of the cost, a deteriorating chimney is a safety issue — leaning stacks and spalled brick can drop material. Early repair is always the cheaper and safer path.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I know if my Burbank chimney needs repair or a full rebuild?
If the damage is limited to a cracked crown, surface mortar loss, or a few spalled bricks near the top, targeted repair and repointing usually solve it. A rebuild is needed when the stack is leaning, the brick is spalling through multiple courses, or the mortar has failed structurally. Emerald Masonry inspects and tells you honestly which one your chimney needs.

### Why is water leaking near my chimney in Burbank?
The most common culprits are a cracked chimney crown, failed mortar joints, deteriorated flashing where the chimney meets the roof, or a missing chimney cap. Any of these lets water into the masonry, where freeze-thaw cycles widen the damage. We find the actual entry point rather than just sealing over symptoms.

### How much does chimney repair cost in Burbank, IL?
It depends on the scope — a crown repair and minor repointing is far less than a partial or full rebuild. Height, access, and the condition of the brick all factor in. We don't quote exact prices online; a free on-site inspection gives you a real number. Emerald Masonry carries a $5,000 project minimum.

### Can you repair a chimney crown without rebuilding the whole chimney?
Yes, and it's one of the most cost-effective repairs we do. The crown is the concrete cap at the top that sheds water off the chimney. When it cracks, water gets into the masonry below. We can rebuild or recast a failed crown on its own when the brick beneath it is still sound.
