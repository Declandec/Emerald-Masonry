## What brick repair includes

**Brick repair** means fixing the bricks themselves and the wall section they belong to. It covers replacing individual units, resetting loose brick, repairing cracks and rebuilding small sections. It is different from **tuckpointing**, which renews only the mortar joints, and from a large-scale **brick replacement**, where whole areas of the wall are taken down and rebuilt.

## Signs your Oak Forest home needs brick repair

- **Flaking or crumbling brick faces**, especially low on the wall or near downspouts.
- **Cracked bricks**, including stair-step cracks through the brick itself.
- **Loose bricks** at steps, corners, sills or the chimney.
- **Rust stains** or cracks above windows and garage doors.
- **White efflorescence** or dark wet patches that keep coming back.
- **Bricks that were patched** with hard gray mortar or caulk.

## Risks of waiting

Damaged brick lets water deeper into the wall. Each winter that water freezes and expands, so one flaking brick spreads to the ones around it and small cracks open up. Loose brick can fall, especially at steps, porches and chimneys. A repair that involves a handful of bricks this year can become a wall section to rebuild later.
