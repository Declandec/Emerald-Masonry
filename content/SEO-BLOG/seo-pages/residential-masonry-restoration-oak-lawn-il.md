## Why bundling repairs makes sense

Every masonry repair has fixed costs: getting ladders or scaffolding up, protecting landscaping and roofs, matching mortar, and cleaning up. Handling the chimney one year, tuckpointing another and lintels a third means paying those costs three times, and often ending up with three slightly different mortar colors. One plan, done in one or two visits, avoids both problems.

When budget does not allow everything at once, we sort the work by priority: safety and active leaks first, then repairs that get worse each winter, then appearance.

## Signs your Oak Lawn home is ready for restoration

- **Several problems** on different parts of the house.
- **Chimney top** with crumbling joints, flaking brick or a cracked crown.
- **Recessed mortar** on the weather side or near the ground.
- **Rust or cracks** above windows and the garage.
- **Cracked limestone sills or steps**, or a stoop pulling away.
- **Old patches** in gray mortar or mismatched brick.
- **Damp basement walls** under a problem area outside.

## Risks of waiting

Each repair delayed lets water keep moving into the wall. Freeze-thaw turns small openings into larger ones, rusting lintels lift and crack the brick, and loose chimney brick becomes a hazard. Waiting also tends to break the work into smaller emergency jobs, which costs more overall.
